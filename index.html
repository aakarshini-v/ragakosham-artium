Carnatic music files every seven-note scale under one of 72 parent ragas, the melakartas, arranged in 12 chakras of six. The first 36 use shuddha madhyamam (M1); the last 36 use prati madhyamam (M2). Every janya raga in this library hangs from one of them.

Notation: S, R1–R3, G1–G3, M1–M2, P, D1–D3, N1–N3; R2 = G1, R3 = G2, D2 = N1, D3 = N2 share a pitch.
